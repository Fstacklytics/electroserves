Ninety-six 400W metal halide fittings were consuming a little over 38kW whenever the
warehouse was occupied, and roughly a fifth of them were failed or failing at any given
time.

## The retrofit

We replaced the fittings with 150W LED high-bays on the existing mounting points,
working from a scissor lift during the warehouse's Saturday shutdown to avoid any
interruption to picking operations.

Measured lux at floor level improved from an average of 180 to 320, which the client's
safety officer had been requesting for two years.

## Emergency lighting

The site had no compliant emergency lighting. We installed maintained emergency
fittings on the escape routes and at each fire point, with a monthly test switch at the
main board and a logbook for the site manager.

## Numbers

Connected lighting load fell from 38.4kW to 14.4kW. At the site's operating hours the
client's own finance team calculated a payback period of just under fourteen months.
