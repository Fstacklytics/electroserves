The building was completed in 1997 and had never been rewired. Twelve of the
twenty-four units were occupied, and the managing agent's brief was unambiguous: no
apartment could lose power overnight.

## The problem

The original installation used rewireable fuse boards with no earth leakage protection
and a shared earth arrangement that had degraded badly. Three separate residents had
reported shocks from taps and appliance casings, which is exactly the symptom that
precedes something far more serious.

Load had also roughly tripled since 1997. Nearly every unit had added air conditioning
on a circuit never designed to carry it.

## How we staged it

We worked two units per week, always a vacant unit paired with an occupied one. The
vacant unit absorbed the disruptive riser work; the occupied unit was rewired between
08:00 and 17:00 and re-energised before the residents returned.

New riser cabling went into fire-rated trunking, with a separate sub-main to each
apartment so a fault in one unit can no longer affect its neighbours.

## Result

All twenty-four units now have individual RCBO protection on every circuit and a
correctly labelled consumer unit. Nine weeks, no overnight outages, and no shock
reports since handover.
