The homeowners in Kigamboni wanted reliable backup power without drilling
through a tiled roof. We designed a hybrid system that prioritises solar
self-consumption and falls back to the grid and batteries during outages.

Work included a structural assessment of the roof, a load study of the home's
essential circuits, and a tidy utility-room installation that keeps the
inverter, batteries and isolation switches accessible for routine maintenance.
