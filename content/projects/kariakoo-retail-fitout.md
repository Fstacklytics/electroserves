Converting a single-supply warehouse into eighteen individually let retail units meant
the electrical installation had to answer a commercial question as much as a technical
one: how does the landlord bill each tenant fairly?

## Distribution and metering

We installed a new three-phase main board with a separately metered sub-board for each
unit. Tenants are balanced across the three phases by expected load rather than by
position in the building, which keeps the phases within 8% of each other at peak.

Each tenant's board is lockable and labelled, so a shopfitter working in one unit
cannot isolate a neighbour.

## Lighting

The arcade circulation space uses LED high-bays on a photocell and timeclock, replacing
the original metal halide fittings. Measured consumption for the common areas fell by
roughly 60%, and the landlord recovers that directly through the service charge.

## Handover

The client received a full schedule of circuits, meter serial numbers mapped to unit
numbers, and thermographic baseline images of the main board for comparison at the
first annual inspection.
