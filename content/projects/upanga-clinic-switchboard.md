The call came in at 16:40 on a Tuesday: burning smell in the plant room, main breaker
tripping on reset. The clinic had a full appointment book the following morning.

## Making it safe

Our technician isolated the supply and confirmed the cause within twenty minutes — a
badly terminated incoming conductor had been arcing intermittently for a long time and
had finally carbonised the busbar insulation.

The board could not be repaired. Attempting to re-energise it would have been
dangerous.

## Overnight replacement

We sourced a replacement board that evening, and a two-technician team worked from
19:00 to 04:30 to install and test it. Every circuit was tested and recorded before we
handed back.

The clinic opened on time at 08:00. This is the case study we point to when clients ask
why we recommend a thermographic inspection twice a year — this failure would have been
visible on a scan months earlier.
