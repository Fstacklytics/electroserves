We were appointed at foundation stage, which is the right time to involve an
electrician and unfortunately the exception rather than the rule.

## First fix

Being on site before the blockwork went up meant conduit could be cast into the slab
and walls rather than chased in afterwards. That saved the main contractor roughly two
weeks of remedial plastering across the project.

We ran structured data cabling to every room at the same time, terminating in a single
comms cabinet in the plant room.

## Second fix and backup

The villa has a 5kVA inverter with a lithium bank covering lighting, refrigeration, the
borehole pump and the comms cabinet. The pump matters more than it sounds: without it,
an extended outage means no water at all.

## Handover

The client received a full circuit schedule, test certificates, and a labelled diagram
of both the main and backup boards mounted inside the plant room door.
