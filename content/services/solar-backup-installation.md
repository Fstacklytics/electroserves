The single most common problem we see with backup systems is that nobody measured the
load before buying the equipment. A 3kVA inverter attached to a battery bank half the
size it needs to be will work for a month and then quietly destroy the batteries.

## We size the system to your actual load

Before we quote anything, we log your consumption. That means identifying which
circuits genuinely need to stay live during an outage — lighting, refrigeration,
routers, a few sockets — and which ones do not.

Almost every client is surprised by the result. Excluding the water heater and air
conditioning from the backup circuit typically halves the system cost without changing
how the outage actually feels.

## What a correct installation includes

- A separate backup distribution board so protected circuits are clearly identified
- Proper DC protection between the panels, the charge controller and the battery bank
- Correct cable sizing for the DC side, which is where most cheap installations fail
- An accessible manual changeover, so the system can be bypassed if the inverter fails
- Labelling and a wiring diagram left on site

## After commissioning

We walk you through the system, show you what the display is telling you, and explain
what a healthy state of charge looks like. You also get the commissioning readings in
writing, which matters when a battery warranty claim comes up two years later.
