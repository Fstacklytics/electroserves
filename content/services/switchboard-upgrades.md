An old rewireable fuse board does two things badly: it does not protect people against
an earth fault, and it gives you no way of knowing which circuit is which.

## Why upgrade

Modern protection detects an earth leakage current of 30mA and disconnects in under
40 milliseconds. A rewireable fuse simply does not do this — it protects the cable,
not the person touching the faulty appliance.

## What the work involves

The property is without power for most of a working day. We transfer each circuit to
the new board one at a time, testing as we go, then carry out a full set of tests on
the completed installation.

You get every circuit clearly labelled, a copy of the test results, and a schedule
showing exactly what each breaker controls — which is worth a great deal the next time
something trips.
