Commercial work is judged on two things: whether the installation is safe, and whether
it lets the business keep trading. We plan around both.

## Survey before quote

We do not quote commercial work from a phone call. A technician visits the site,
measures the actual load on each phase, photographs the existing distribution and
identifies what has to be isolated during the work. You get a fixed written quote and
a schedule that says which circuits are down and when.

## Keeping you trading

For occupied premises we work in phases, usually starting outside business hours for
anything that requires a main isolation. For a retail unit that typically means the
noisy and disruptive work happens overnight, and the tidy-up and testing happen during
the day while you trade normally.

## Maintenance that prevents the callout

Around half our commercial clients move onto a planned maintenance contract after the
first project. A thermographic scan of the main switchboard twice a year finds the loose
termination that would otherwise become an unplanned shutdown — and it costs a fraction
of a day's lost trading.
