Replacing a failed inverter costs several million shillings. Repairing one is usually
a few hundred thousand, and in most cases the fault is a handful of components rather
than the whole board.

## How the diagnosis works

Bring the unit to our Kurasini workshop, or we collect it for larger equipment. We
charge a flat diagnosis fee and credit it in full against the repair if you go ahead.

Within two working days you get a written report: what failed, why it failed, what the
repair costs, and — importantly — whether we think it is worth repairing. We say so
plainly when a unit has reached the end of its economic life.

## What we repair most often

Inverters and UPS units make up the majority of our workshop volume. The common
failures are predictable: bulging electrolytic capacitors on the DC bus, failed
MOSFETs in the output stage, and cooked charging circuits caused by a battery bank
that was never correctly matched to the inverter in the first place.

We fix the fault, then we tell you what caused it. Repairing an inverter without
addressing an undersized battery bank simply means seeing the same unit again in
eight months.

## Warranty

Every repair carries a 90-day warranty covering the work we did and the components we
fitted. If the same fault returns in that window, we repair it again at no charge.
