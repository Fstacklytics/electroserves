If you can smell burning, see scorching around a socket, or your main breaker will not
stay on, switch off at the main isolator and call us. Those three symptoms are the ones
that should never wait until the morning.

## What happens when you call

You speak to a technician, not a call centre. We ask what you can see and smell, and
we tell you immediately whether it is safe to stay in the building and what to isolate
before we arrive.

We quote the callout charge on that call, before we dispatch. There are no surprise
out-of-hours multipliers added to the invoice afterwards.

## Made safe, then repaired

The first job on site is always to make the installation safe. That may mean isolating
a single circuit so the rest of the property keeps working, and returning in daylight
with the right parts to complete a proper repair.

We would rather isolate one circuit at 3am and fix it properly at 9am than improvise a
permanent repair in the dark.

## Coverage

We cover Dar es Salaam and the surrounding areas including Kigamboni, Bagamoyo Road and
Kisarawe. Response time targets are 90 minutes within the city and up to three hours
for outlying areas, and we tell you the realistic figure when you call.
