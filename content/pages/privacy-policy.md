This policy explains what personal information ElectroServes Tanzania Limited collects
through this website, why we collect it, and what we do with it. It is written to comply
with the Tanzania Personal Data Protection Act, 2022.

## Who we are

ElectroServes Tanzania Limited, Plot 42, Nyerere Road, Kurasini, Dar es Salaam,
Tanzania. For any question about this policy or your personal data, contact us at
hello@electroserves.co.tz.

## What we collect

**Information you give us through the contact form.** Your name, email address, phone
number if you choose to provide it, the type of service you need, and the content of
your message.

**Technical information collected automatically.** Our web server records the IP address
of each request, the page requested, and the time. This is standard server logging and
is used only for security and diagnosing faults.

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or third-party analytics that
profile visitors.

## Why we collect it

We use contact form submissions for one purpose: to respond to your enquiry and, if you
engage us, to carry out the work. We do not use your details for marketing and we do not
add you to a mailing list.

Server logs are used to investigate security incidents and technical faults.

## Our legal basis

We process contact form data on the basis of your consent, which you give by ticking the
consent box before submitting the form. You may withdraw that consent at any time by
contacting us.

## How long we keep it

Contact form submissions are delivered to us by email and are **not stored in any
database on this website**. We retain the resulting email correspondence for twelve
months from your last contact with us, unless you become a client — in which case
project correspondence is retained for seven years to meet tax and professional
obligations.

Server logs are retained for thirty days and then deleted automatically.

## Who we share it with

Nobody, with two narrow exceptions:

- Our email service provider, which transmits and stores the message on our behalf.
- Where we are legally required to disclose information to a competent authority.

We do not sell personal information under any circumstances.

## How we protect it

This website is served exclusively over HTTPS. Contact form submissions are transmitted
over an encrypted connection. The form is protected against automated abuse by rate
limiting and a honeypot field. Access to our email accounts is restricted to the staff
who need it, and is protected by multi-factor authentication.

## Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022, you may ask us to:

- confirm what personal data we hold about you, and provide a copy;
- correct any personal data that is inaccurate;
- delete your personal data where we have no continuing obligation to retain it;
- stop processing your data, by withdrawing your consent.

Write to hello@electroserves.co.tz and we will respond within thirty days. There is no
charge for these requests.

## Children

This website is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from anyone under eighteen.

## Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Material
changes will be summarised here for at least ninety days.
